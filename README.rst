Where is Automatic mode?
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Automatic mode will require users to sign in with their steam account to download the correct manifest using their license of the game. Not only do I not know how to do this yet (although I will be learning soon), but it is a small security risk for those who don't trust my code. I will be making it work, but finding the right way to implement it is what I care most about. Manual works just fine and still doesn't require much user input, so I say it's a win for the moment.

TLDR; it is coming, but there will be warnings about privacy and safety for those who care about that, like me.
